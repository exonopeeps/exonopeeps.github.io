How the afternoon runs

1:00

Doors & color coding

Check in and identify your primary offer and your primary need — this sets your color-coded identifier for the room.

1:20

Orientation

The hosts walk through the exchange rule, the timing, and what every round has to produce.

1:30

Rapid rounds

Structured 1:1 conversations. Each person shares what they do, what they need, and what they can give — both sides commit to an exchange before moving on.

2:30

Host panel

Community × economic infrastructure — a short conversation between AllPeeP and Exonome leaders, with invited partners, on how trust becomes durable exchange.

3:00

Reconnection

Find the people you most want to keep talking to and agree on one concrete next step.

3:15

Activate

Hands-on time to stand up your own Discovery exchange with Exonome, so the offer and need you brought stays live after the room empties.

4:00

Close

 

Every conversation ends with an exchange

No one leaves with only a stack of business cards. Each round has to close with one of the following.

Constructive feedback that strengthens an idea, offer, or approach
A relevant introduction or referral
A resource, service, or solution
A potential partnership or collaboration
A specific next step for continuing the relationship
